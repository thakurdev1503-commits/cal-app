Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

cal. is a calorie and macro tracker made by Dev Thakur in Delhi, India. This page explains in plain language what the app stores about you, who helps process it, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and a hashed (scrambled) version of your password. We never store your actual password.
  • Body profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and goal. These are used only to calculate your calorie and macro targets.
  • Your logs: meals (name, calories, protein, carbs, fat), weight entries, water intake, and workouts.
  • Settings: your preferences for the streak reminder and notifications.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or payment details, and we do not run advertising.

How it is used

Your data is used only to run the app: to log you in, show your history and progress, and calculate your targets. We do not sell your data and do not share it for advertising.

Services that help us run cal.

  • Supabase stores the database (hosted in Mumbai, India).
  • Render runs the server.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) estimates nutrition. When you describe a meal, take a meal photo, estimate a workout, or request an insight, that text, photo, or a short summary of your recent logs is sent to Anthropic to produce the answer. Photos are not saved on our servers.
  • Sentry receives technical error reports when something breaks, so bugs can be fixed. We do not send your meals or personal details to it on purpose.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile and all your logs from our database.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Profile → Your data → Download my data.
  • Delete your meals only: Profile → Delete all my meals.
  • Delete your account and all data: Profile → Delete my account. You will be asked for your password. This cannot be undone.

You can also email us to ask for a copy, a correction, or deletion of your data.

Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and every request only returns your own data. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Not medical advice

cal. gives estimates, not medical advice. AI calorie and nutrient values can be wrong. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before making big changes to your diet, especially if you have a health condition.

Children

cal. is not meant for people under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
